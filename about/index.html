About

Octrie builds practical tools for operators.

Octrie exists for people who need useful decision support, not generic spreadsheets or overcomplicated software.

Position

Source-backed, usable, and built for action.

The first Octrie tools focus on real estate decision-making: rehab estimates, acquisition workflows, scripts, checklists, and deal-screening resources.

The goal is simple: help operators move faster with cleaner assumptions, better conversations, and repeatable workflows.

Tools

Calculators and resources people can use to make better decisions.

Library

Articles, videos, notes, and methodology behind the work.

Solutions

Custom builds for businesses that need private tools or internal workflows.
