About

We find operational problems and build the tools that fix them.

Octrie is an AI-powered solutions business. We work inside your operation to understand what's costing you — then design and build the custom tools, automations, and workflows that fix it.

What we do

Custom solutions, not adapted software.

Most business software is built for a generic version of your industry. Octrie builds for your specific operation — the real inputs, the real constraints, and the decisions your team actually makes.

We use AI-assisted research and development to move fast and build precisely. That means less back-and-forth and more time solving the actual problem.

Diagnose

We start by mapping the workflow: where decisions get stuck, what's tracked in the wrong place, and what would need to be true for the operation to run cleaner.

Build

Once scope is clear, we design and build the solution — calculator, script, dashboard, automation, or internal workflow — to match your exact requirements.

Hand Off

You get a working solution you own: documented, explained, and ready to use. No platform dependency, no ongoing subscription.
